Educational Guide

The Sunnah of Takfeen

Step-by-step guidance according to authentic Islamic jurisprudence for funeral committees and grieving relatives.

01

Preparation

The table is prepared. The Lifafa sheet is laid first, followed by Izar and Qamees. The cloths are perfumed with Bukhoor or camphor an odd number of times.

02

Placement

Following Ghusl, the deceased is dried gently and placed respectfully on the prepared cloths. Camphor is placed on the seven points of Sujood.

03

Wrapping

The Qamees covers the torso. The left side of the Izar wraps over the body, then the right. For women, the Khimar covers the hair and the Seenah-band secures the chest.

04

Fastening

The outermost Lifafa sheet is wrapped around the body. Cotton tie ribbons are fastened at the head, waist, and feet to prevent shifting during transit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, sincere answers regarding Sunnah compliance, shipping, and sizing.

Are Wasim Trader Kaffans 100% compliant with Islamic Sunnah?
Yes, strictly. Our garments adhere to classical Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh) across all four Sunni schools. The men's set contains exactly 3 unstitched white cotton pieces (Lifafa, Izar, Qamees) plus pre-cut ties. The women's set contains exactly 5 pieces (Lifafa, Izar, Qamees, Khimar/Aurni, Seenah-band) plus pre-cut ties for comprehensive modesty.
How fast can you dispatch in an emergency Janazah situation?
We understand that Islamic burials must take place as swiftly as possible. For emergency requests, contact our dedicated WhatsApp line immediately. We offer expedited tracked courier dispatch for urgent family needs across USA, Australia, and New Zealand.
How do the pre-cut ties (Ribat) work?
Every set includes 3 to 4 pre-cut pure cotton tie strips (60″ × 2″ each). Volunteers use them to fasten the shroud at the head, midsection, and feet during Janazah transit. They are untied or loosened upon placement in the Qabr (grave) according to Sunnah.
Can donors purchase sets as Sadaqah Jariyah for their community?
Yes, many community members purchase batches of sets to donate directly to their local community or funeral volunteers as continuous ongoing charity (*Sadaqah Jariyah*). We can deliver the shipment directly to your nominated destination.
