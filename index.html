Educational Guide

The Sunnah of Takfeen (Shrouding)

A respectful step-by-step summary according to Islamic traditions, giving clarity and solace to family members and Janazah teams.

01

Preparation & Perfume

The table is prepared. The Lifafa sheet is laid out first, followed by Izar and Qamees. The cloths are perfumed with incense (Bukhoor) or natural camphor an odd number of times (three times is Sunnah).

02

Placement of the Body

Following the final rinse of Ghusl, the deceased is dried gently and placed respectfully upon the cloths. Camphor or perfume is applied to the seven points of Sujood (forehead, nose, palms, knees, and feet).

03

Sequential Wrapping

The Qamees covers the torso. Next, the left side of the Izar is folded over the body, followed by the right side. For women, the hair is arranged in three braids, the Khimar covers the head, and the Seenah-band secures the chest.

04

Fastening with Pre-Cut Ties

The outermost Lifafa sheet is wrapped (left side first, then right). The pre-cut cotton tie ribbons are fastened at the head, waist, and feet to prevent shifting during transit, and loosened upon placement in the grave.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, sincere answers regarding Sunnah compliance, wholesale carton logistics, and sample kits.

How does the Free Masjid Sample Kit work? +
We provide verified Mosque Imams, Board Members, and Janazah Funeral Coordinators in the USA, Australia, and New Zealand with a complimentary, complete sample kit. Simply submit the form above with your organization details. Our team verifies the masjid information and dispatches the sample for your committee to inspect the fabric and pre-cut dimensions firsthand.
Are Wasim Trader Kaffans 100% compliant with Islamic Sunnah? +
Yes, strictly. Our garments adhere to classical Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh) across all four Sunni schools. The men's set contains exactly 3 unstitched white cotton pieces (Lifafa, Izar, Qamees) plus pre-cut ties. The women's set contains exactly 5 pieces (Lifafa, Izar, Qamees, Khimar/Aurni, Seenah-band) plus pre-cut ties for comprehensive modesty.
How are wholesale cartons packaged and shipped? +
Wholesale cartons are packed in heavy-duty, moisture-sealed master boxes containing 20, 50, or 100 individually sealed sets. You can customize the ratio of Men's vs. Women's sets. Shipping is calculated based on destination and carton volume with tracked express freight directly to your masjid.
Can community donors sponsor wholesale cartons as Sadaqah Jariyah? +
Yes, many community members purchase batches of 20 or 50 sets to donate directly to their local masjid mortuary as continuous ongoing charity (*Sadaqah Jariyah*). We can deliver the shipment directly to your nominated mosque with your dedication notice.
How do the pre-cut ties (Ribat) work? +
Every set includes 3 to 4 pre-cut pure cotton tie strips (60″ × 2″ each). Volunteers use them to fasten the shroud at the head, midsection, and feet during Janazah transit. They are untied or loosened upon placement in the Qabr (grave) according to Sunnah.
Do you accommodate custom sizing for exceptionally tall individuals? +
Yes. Our standard Large and Extra Large dimensions generously shroud individuals up to 6'5"+. If your committee requires custom lengths beyond 130 inches for specific needs, mention this in your inquiry and our cutting workshop will prepare them accordingly.
