# Religious Guidelines: The Sunnah & Fiqh of Takfeen (Islamic Shrouding)

## 1. Theological Importance of the Kaffan

In Islam, preparing the deceased (*Mayyit*) for burial is a collective communal obligation (*Fard Kifayah*). If a portion of the community fulfills it, the obligation is lifted from the rest; if neglected, the entire community bears responsibility.

Takfeen (shrouding) is the second major step following Ghusl (ritual washing) and preceding Salat al-Janazah (funeral prayer) and Dafn (burial). Shrouding must be carried out with utmost dignity, gentleness, and reverence.

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said:
> **"When one of you shrouds his brother, let him make his shroud good (and clean)."**  
> *(Sahih Muslim 943)*

And regarding the choice of fabric:
> **"Wear your white garments, for they are among your best garments, and shroud your dead in them."**  
> *(Sunan Abi Dawud 4061, Jami` at-Tirmidhi 994 — Sahih)*

---

## 2. Key Islamic Fiqh Principles for Kaffan Fabric

1. **Color (White is Sunnah & Mustahabb)**: White is universally recommended across all four major schools of Fiqh (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali).
2. **Material (Pure Cotton)**: Natural cotton is the preferred material. Silk (*Hareer*) is prohibited for men in life and in death; it is also disapproved (*Makruh*) or discouraged for women in death to avoid extravagance.
3. **Simplicity & Moderation**: The shroud should be clean, respectable, and cover the *Awrah* (private parts) thoroughly, but free from boastful extravagance, luxury embroidery, or excessive ostentation.
4. **Number of Cloths**:
   - **For Men (Sunnah: 3 Pieces)**:
     Aisha (RA) narrated:  
     > *"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ was shrouded in three white Sahooli cotton garments from Yemen, among which there was neither a shirt nor a turban."*  
     > *(Sahih al-Bukhari 1264, Sahih Muslim 941)*
   - **For Women (Sunnah: 5 Pieces)**:
     Umm 'Atiyyah (RA) narrated regarding the shrouding of the Prophet's daughter:  
     > *"He gave us an Izar, then a Qamees, then a Khimar, then a Lifafa, then wrapped another sheet over it."*  
     > *(Sunan Abi Dawud 3157, Sunan an-Nasa'i)*

## 3. Garments Breakdown & Sequence of Shrouding

### A. Men's Shroud (3 Pieces)
1. **Lifafa (Outermost Large Sheet)**:
   - Spread on the washing table first.
   - Scented with incense / perfume (*Ithr*, *Bukhoor*, or camphor/Kafoor) an odd number of times (3 times is Sunnah).
2. **Izar (Inner Sheet)**:
   - Spread directly on top of the Lifafa.
3. **Qamees (Shirt / Tunic)**:
   - Placed on top of the Izar.
   - Has a slit for the head to slide through; the back section rests under the deceased and the front covers the chest down to the feet.
4. **Wrapping Procedure**:
   - The deceased is placed on the prepared cloths.
   - Scent/camphor is placed on the seven points of Sujood (forehead/nose, palms, knees, feet).
   - The Qamees covers the front.
   - The left side of the Izar is folded over the right, then the right side over the left.
   - The left side of the Lifafa is folded over the right, then the right over the left.
   - The head and foot ends are tied with cloth strips to prevent uncovering during transport, and loosened upon placement in the grave.

---

### B. Women's Shroud (5 Pieces)
1. **Lifafa (Outermost Sheet)**: Spread out first.
2. **Seenah-band (Chest Band)**: Laid out across the torso section.
3. **Izar (Loincloth / Lower Sheet)**: Laid out on top.
4. **Qamees (Upper Shirt / Tunic)**: Placed on top with front folded back.
5. **Khimar / Aurni (Head Veil)**: Placed beneath the head to drape over the hair after parting into three braids.
6. **Wrapping Procedure**:
   - The deceased female's hair is arranged in three braids (one at the front, two at the sides) and laid behind her back over the Qamees.
   - The Khimar covers her head, face, and hair.
   - The Qamees is folded down over her body.
   - The Izar is wrapped (left then right).
   - The Seenah-band is wrapped tightly over the chest and tied under the armpits or down to the waist.
   - The Lifafa is wrapped around the entire body, and strips are tied at the head, waist, and feet.

## 4. Why Pre-Cut Wasim Trader Sets Honor the Sunnah

- **Pre-measured Dimensions**: Prevents cloth from being either too short (compromising modesty) or unnecessarily wasteful (*Israaf*).
- **Hygienic Sealed Packets**: Prevents contamination or dust prior to the sacred ritual.
- **Immediate Readiness**: Eliminates scrambling for scissors and rulers when grieving relatives should be engaged in Du'a (supplication) and Janazah arrangements.
